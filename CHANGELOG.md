# Changelog

The site version shows at the bottom right of the page. New features bump the middle number, fixes and small
tweaks bump the last one. Adding or updating characters doesn't change the site version.

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-10)

First numbered version. What the site does at this point:

- Character cards with up to 6 pictures (arrows, dots, swipe), ten themes with their own edge stickers.
- A story-style Guild Wrapped per character, with slide arrows, from their `/gf export` stats.
- Memories: up to 3 clips per character (files or YouTube), opening in a big player.
- Full-screen viewer for pictures (uncropped originals) and clips, sized to fit the screen (up to 84%).
- Character versions: updates keep the old version; a picker in the Wrapped switches between them, and the
  latest version gets a "Since last time" growth slide.
- "Add your character" guide and a "Make an entry" builder with an update mode.
- Version stamp and this label at the bottom right.
